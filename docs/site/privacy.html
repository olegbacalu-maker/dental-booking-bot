Programul are două legături cu Google, fiecare pornită doar de clinică: conectarea contului Google al clinicii, din care programul numai citește (mai jos), și calendarele medicilor, în care programul scrie programările (mai jos, separat).

Contul Google al clinicii. Conectarea există în DentPilot™ Plus, în DentPilot™ Premium și în perioada de probă: un utilizator cu rolul Director sau Recepție apasă în program „Conectează contul Google” și aprobă accesul pe pagina Google, în browserul calculatorului.

Numai pentru citire, cu permisiunile Google calendar.calendarlist.readonly (lista calendarelor) și calendar.events.readonly (evenimentele): lista calendarelor contului conectat — numele și identificatorul fiecăruia (la calendarul principal identificatorul este adresa de e-mail a contului) —, ca clinica să aleagă calendarul citit; evenimentele calendarului ales, de acum până la un an înainte: titlul, data, ora și durata, descrierea, locul, invitații (numele și adresele lor de e-mail), starea evenimentului (confirmat sau anulat) și identificatorii lui tehnici. Programul nu accesează alte date ale contului Google — nici e-mailurile, nici contactele, nici fișierele, nici parola — și, prin această conectare, nu scrie, nu modifică și nu șterge nimic în calendarul clinicii.

Numai pentru funcția vizibilă în program: evenimentele apar în fila „Google Calendar”, unde personalul clinicii le vede și le mută în jurnalul programărilor; dacă clinica a pornit mutarea automată, programul mută singur evenimentele în care recunoaște medicul și pacientul (la nevoie creează fișa pacientului, cu numele și telefonul din eveniment), iar celelalte rămân pentru personal. Punctele „Limited Use” și „Modificări” de mai sus se aplică ambelor legături.

In English — Google user data in the DentPilot app. DentPilot is a desktop program installed on the clinic's Windows computer. It has two separate Google features, each turned on only by the clinic: the clinic's Google account (read-only, below) and the doctors' calendars (the program writes into a calendar it creates, further below). In DentPilot Plus, DentPilot Premium and during the trial, a user with the Director or Recepție role may connect the clinic's Google account (button “Conectează contul Google”) to show the events of one Google Calendar in the program's “Google Calendar” tab, where clinic staff move them into the appointment journal; if the clinic turns on automatic moving, the program moves the events in which it recognizes the doctor and the patient by itself.

  • Data accessed: read-only, with the scopes calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events.readonly: the list of the account's calendars (name and ID; the primary calendar's ID is the account's e-mail address), so the clinic can choose one, and the events of the chosen calendar from now to one year ahead (title, date, time and duration, description, location, attendees' names and e-mail addresses, status, technical identifiers). No other Google data — no mail, contacts, files or password. Through this connection the program never writes, changes or deletes anything in the clinic's calendar.
  • Use: only for the clinic's appointment journal: to display the events in the program's Google Calendar tab, to flag events changed or cancelled in Google, and to move events into the journal — by clinic staff or, if the clinic turns on automatic moving (off until then), by the program for events in which it recognizes the doctor and the patient (creating the patient record from the event's name and phone when there is none). The calendar is read every 10 minutes while the program runs and on “Verifică acum”. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, the developer's analytics or any other purpose, and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.
  • Storage and protection: only on the clinic's computer — events in the program's local database (included in the backups the clinic makes); the OAuth token in a separate file, encrypted with Windows DPAPI and bound to the Windows account, never in the database or the backups. The program talks to Google directly over HTTPS; the sign-in runs in the system browser on Google's page. The developer's servers never receive tokens or calendar data. Only users with full-journal access (Director and Recepție roles) see the tab.
  • Retention and deletion: past events and events more than one year ahead are not fetched; the text of events not moved into the journal is erased automatically 7 days after their date; moving an event erases its text from the tab (its title and the beginning of its description go into the comment of the new appointment); “Șterge tot din Google” deletes the events not moved at once; erasing a patient's data under Moldovan Law 195/2024 also erases their events.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold and is not shared with or transferred to the developer or anyone else, with one exception the clinic controls: an appointment created from an event is an ordinary appointment of the clinic, and if the clinic uses the SMS module and has chosen so (in the booking dialog, or in the tab's setting for appointments moved automatically or in a batch), an SMS reminder — the phone number and a text with the clinic's name, date and time, no patient name or treatment — passes through the developer's server, which does not keep it, to the SMS operator (section 4). The developer may see the clinic's screen only during a Windows Quick Assist session the clinic requests, in its presence, without copying or keeping anything.
  • Revoking access: “Deconectează” in the program (revokes the token at Google and deletes it from the computer) or myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Limited Use: DentPilot's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Doctors' calendars (DentPilot Premium). In DentPilot Premium the clinic may connect each doctor's own Google account so that the doctor sees their appointments on their phone. A user with the Director or Recepție role starts it from the doctor's card (button “Conectează Google”) on the computer where the program is installed; the doctor signs in to their own Google account in the system browser and grants access.

  • Scope: a single one, calendar.app.created — the program can create its own secondary calendar in the doctor's account and read and write events only in that calendar. It cannot see or change the account's other calendars or events, and accesses no mail, contacts, files or password.
  • Data written to Google: one calendar named “DentPilot” with the clinic's name and the doctor's name, and in it the doctor's appointments from 30 days back to 90 days ahead: date, time, duration, room, and a note that appointments are changed only in DentPilot. The clinic's director chooses the level of detail: time only (the default — no patient data), the patient's initials and record number, or the patient's name and the service. The two levels with patient data require an explicit warning dialog with a checkbox, and the choice is logged in the program. The patient's phone number, national ID, medical history, diagnosis, treatment plan and amounts are never sent. A new or changed appointment reaches the calendar within 5 minutes and on “Sincronizează acum”; cancelled appointments are removed and appointments older than 30 days are deleted from it.
  • Google user data received and its use: only about the calendar the program created — its ID, name and description, the e-mail address of the account that owns it, and the IDs and status of the events the program wrote. It is used only to keep that calendar up to date and to show in the doctor's card which account is connected. It is not used for advertising, profiling, the developer's analytics or any other purpose, and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.
  • Storage and protection: each doctor's OAuth token, the account's e-mail address and the calendar ID are kept only on the clinic's computer, in a separate file; the token is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and bound to the Windows account; the file is never in the database or the backups. The program's local database keeps only technical identifiers of the calendar and of the events written, without patient data. The program talks to Google directly over HTTPS. The developer's servers never receive tokens, appointments or calendar data.
  • Sharing: appointments go from the clinic's computer directly to Google, into the doctor's account. Google user data is not sold and is not shared with or transferred to the developer or anyone else.
  • Stopping and deletion: “Deconectează” in the doctor's card deletes the calendar, with all its appointments, from the doctor's Google account and deletes the token from the computer; archiving the doctor does the same. The program does not revoke the app's grant at Google, because that would also end the same Google account's other connections with the app; the doctor can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. When DentPilot Premium is no longer active or the program is in read-only mode, the program removes all appointments, past ones included, from the calendar and stops updating it: the calendar stays empty. Erasing a patient's data under Moldovan Law 195/2024 also removes their data from the doctors' calendars at the next update.
  • The Limited Use statements above apply to this feature as well.
